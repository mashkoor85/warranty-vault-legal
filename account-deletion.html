Delete your Warranty Vault account

Warranty Vault by MashkoorDev. Effective date: 13 October 2026.

From the app

  1. Sign in to the Warranty Vault account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings and the account controls.
  3. Select Delete account and confirm the displayed warning.

Account deletion enters a 30-day recovery period. Sign in during that period and use the offered account restore flow if you change your mind. After expiry, recovery is unavailable and permanent cleanup proceeds.

Without the app

Email mashkoor85@gmail.com with the subject “Warranty Vault account deletion request,” preferably from the Google email address used for your account. Tell us you want your Warranty Vault account and associated data deleted. Do not send your password, verification codes, identity documents, or receipt contents.

We may need to verify that you control the account before acting. We will respond to your deletion request within 30 days. This response timeframe is separate from the 30-day recovery window, which begins when deletion is requested in the service.

What is deleted and retained

The implemented cleanup removes your account identity and owned vault groups, purchase/warranty records and associated receipt data after the recovery period. Shared records and receipts belonging to groups owned by other users may remain available to those groups. Exported backups and other users' independent copies are not removed by deleting your account.

Permanent cleanup may take additional processing time after the 30-day recovery window. Contact us for information about the status of your deletion request.

Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your cloud account. Manage any exported backups or other copies separately.
