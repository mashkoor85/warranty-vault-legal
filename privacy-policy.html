Warranty Vault Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026. Developer: MashkoorDev. Contact: mashkoor85@gmail.com.

Information you provide

Warranty Vault manages product purchases, warranty coverage, dates, prices, currencies, retailers, notes, groups, and receipt images or documents you choose to add. These may contain personal information. Camera and file-selection features let you provide receipts; avoid including information you do not need to store.

Accounts and cloud storage

Google sign-in identifies your account. Your name, account identifiers, email, authentication/session information, device registrations and synchronization metadata support access control and syncing. Supabase provides authentication, database and receipt storage. Cloud features transmit your vault records and attachments to the configured service. Copies are also stored on your device for offline use.

How information is used and shared

We use this information to provide your vault, reminders, receipt processing, backups, synchronization, sharing, account recovery and deletion. Records placed in shared groups are available to the group's authorized members according to their permissions. Do not share receipts containing information you do not want those members to access.

Receipt recognition

The app uses bundled Google ML Kit text recognition on your device to suggest receipt fields. Review suggestions before saving. Receipt files may separately be uploaded for cloud storage and group sharing; on-device recognition does not mean the receipt stays only on your device. The ML Kit SDK also sends diagnostic and usage information to Google, including device/app information, an installation identifier, configuration, processing latency and errors. This telemetry is separate from the receipt text recognized on your device.

Advertising

The app includes Google AdMob and Google's consent tools. Advertising services may process identifiers, IP address, device/app information and advertising interactions and diagnostics for delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and related purposes. Available consent and privacy choices depend on your location and service configuration. Use the app's advertising privacy controls to review available choices.

See Google's privacy policy and Supabase's privacy policy for these providers' practices.

Retention and deletion

Active vault records remain available until deleted. The implemented cloud lifecycle provides a 30-day recovery window for account deletion and supported trashed records. Permanent cleanup occurs after the recovery window; it may take additional processing time. Receipts contributed to groups owned by other users may be retained with those groups. Copies exported to backups or obtained by other members must be managed separately.

See Request account deletion for the in-app and contact routes.

Your choices

You can review and edit records, manage group sharing and registered devices, export supported backups, review advertising privacy choices, and request account deletion. Removing the app alone does not request deletion of cloud data. Local exports outside the app are under your control.

Age audience

Warranty Vault is intended for users aged 13 and above. You can choose an advertising age range: under 18, 18 or older, or prefer not to say. Only the chosen range is stored on your device; the app does not collect your date of birth for this choice. You can change the range in Settings. Users under 18 and those who prefer not to say receive conservative age-restricted advertising treatment. For adults, Google determines which regional consent message and advertising privacy choices are required. Where available, you can reopen Advertising privacy choices in Settings to change or withdraw consent. These choices do not remove the data processing described above. Contact us if you have a concern about a child's personal information.

Security

The app uses authenticated cloud access and transport protections. No system guarantees absolute security.

Questions and updates

Contact the developer at the address above for privacy questions or requests. We will update this page when practices change and publish its effective date.
